Courses

Every course, and what it is for.

44 courses · 120 units · one unit is three hours of work a week for fifteen weeks · every syllabus open

How It Fits

One degree, eight semesters. The first four are the same for everyone: the books, the mathematics and the science, code, writing, and the Workshop every semester. That is the Foundation, and it is where you find out what you are good at. At the end of Year 2 you declare a concentration (Media, Design, or Engineering), take four depth studios in it and one from outside it, spend a term inside a working organization, and defend a capstone in public.

Every course below says what it is for and what you make in it, because every course ends in a made thing that goes into your Portfolio. The full syllabus (hours, materials, how it is assessed, the unassisted examination) is on each course's page. A course's fifteen days are its spine: one page a week, about ten minutes to read, each opening that week's work. The hours are the course. The Schedule shows what a week and a semester look like; the Path dates every day.
