The words that open the degree and close each semester. Day 0 (/begin/) is the Commitment and three questions; each semester ends at a gate that asks them again, plus what you made and what changed. Every answer is kept in the learner's Notebook under the page's lesson id and the question id, so the same question can be read across four years.

commitment

heading
The Commitment
paragraphs
  • Everything here is open. You can read any day of any course without an account and without asking. If that is what you want, go ahead; the doors stay open.
  • If you want the whole education, there is one condition: trust the system. Do the days in order. Do the reading behind the doors when it pulls you. Make the things, and finish them. Write your answers down. Show up for the gates. The sequence is the point; it was built to be walked, not browsed.
  • If you commit to that, we commit to the other half. The next day will be there every Monday. The work will be real. And the whole thing is built so that you come out of it with a mind ready to live in the present, and in whatever comes after it.
