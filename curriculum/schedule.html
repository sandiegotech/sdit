The Schedule

A semester, one day at a time.

15-week semesters · five days a week · about ten minutes a day · self-paced

The Shape
A semester

15 weeks, five or six courses — the same courses all semester, so each one builds day over day.

A week

One course per day. Monday is always the books, Friday is always the Workshop. The middle of the week changes with the semester — each semester's week is laid out below.

A day

One sitting, about ten minutes: one thing to experience, one idea, one question to carry. Then you're done.

Your pace

"Tomorrow" means whenever you come back. The schedule is a shape, not a clock — the site remembers where you left off on this device.
